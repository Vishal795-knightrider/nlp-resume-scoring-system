    Algorithmic Architecture

    How ResumeFit Analyzes Your Resume

    Built with robust natural language processing and vector space geometry — zero hallucination, pure mathematical scoring.

    📄

    In-Memory PDF Parsing

    Extracts text page-by-page directly in memory via pypdf. Your documents are never permanently stored on server disk.

    🧹

    Text Preprocessing

    Normalizes casing, strips non-alphabetical punctuation, and removes high-frequency English stopwords to isolate domain signals.

    📐

    TF-IDF Vectorization

    Weights terms based on Term Frequency and Inverse Document Frequency, magnifying high-impact domain competencies over common filler words.

    🌐

    Cosine Similarity Metric

    Computes the geometric cosine of the angle between resume and JD multidimensional vectors, providing length-independent matching.

    🔍

    Keyword Gap Analysis

    Performs set-theoretic differential analysis to spotlight critical qualifications present in the JD but missing in your resume.

    💡

    Heuristic Recommendations

    Synthesizes structural section coverage, score thresholds, and missing skills into actionable suggestions to optimize ATS pass rates.
