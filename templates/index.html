    How It Works

    The system compares your resume and job description using natural language processing techniques.

    01
    Resume Text Extraction

    Extracts text page-by-page directly in memory using pypdf. No uploaded files are written to disk.

    02
    Text Preprocessing

    Converts text to lowercase, removes punctuation and filters common English stopwords.

    03
    TF-IDF Vectorization

    Calculates term frequency and inverse document frequency weights across the shared vocabulary.

    04
    Cosine Similarity

    Measures the cosine angle between the resume and job vectors in multi-dimensional space.

    05
    Keyword Comparison

    Identifies overlapping terms and isolates significant job keywords missing from your resume.

    06
    Resume Suggestions

    Generates targeted suggestions based on score thresholds, missing technical skills and structure.
